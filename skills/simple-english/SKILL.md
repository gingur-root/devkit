# Simple English

Written from the gingur-root SDLC writing rules and the I Have ADHD skill
(`ayghri/i-have-adhd@723af7d`). Pragmatic mode: exact names, commands and
paths stay as they are, even when they're not simple words.

1. **One idea per sentence.** Short sentences, everyday words.
2. **Start each section with what the reader does or gets.** Background
   comes after, only if it changes what they do.
3. **Number procedures.** One action per step; put the command first.
4. **Say why once.** A rule with its reason is followed; a bare rule is
   argued with. Don't repeat the reason elsewhere.
5. **Name things the same way every time.** A second name for one thing reads
   as a second thing.
6. **Cut what doesn't change behavior.** History, alternatives not taken, and
   restated context go.
